Need help with Puzzlr? You are in the right place. Puzzlr is a collection of 11 offline puzzle games: word games, number games, and logic grids, all in one app.

Contact

Email us at puzzlr-support@koraykural.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked

  • Does it work offline? Yes. Every game runs fully offline, with no account needed.
  • Do I need an account? No. There is no login and no sign up.
  • How do I send feedback? Open Settings, then "Share feedback."
  • How do I report a bug? Email us with your device model and a short note on what happened.
  • How do I turn off analytics? Open Settings and switch off "Share anonymous usage data."
