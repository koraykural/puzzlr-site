Puzzlr

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Türkçe

Puzzlr is a collection of offline puzzle games. We built it to be simple and private. There are no accounts, no logins, and no ads. This policy explains the limited data the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Anonymous usage and diagnostics. Puzzlr uses Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to understand how the games are used and to fix problems. This includes app usage events (for example, which game you opened, whether a round was finished, and which garden item you bought), a random app-instance identifier, and technical details such as your device model, operating system version, app version, and the general region derived from your IP address.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a diagnostic report so we can fix the issue.
  • Feedback you choose to send. Puzzlr sometimes asks how you are finding a game. If you answer, we store what you sent: the rating you picked, the note you typed if you wrote one, which game it was about, and your app version, platform, and language. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. Please leave personal details out of the note. We do not want them, and we cannot reply to it. If you need an answer, email us instead.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or account. Puzzlr has no login and never asks who you are.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No precise location.
  • No payment information. Puzzlr has nothing to buy with real money, so there is no card, billing address, or purchase history.
  • Your game progress is never sent to us. On iPhone and iPad it syncs through your own iCloud account, which we cannot read.

iCloud on iPhone and iPad

Puzzlr saves your play history, your streaks and any unfinished puzzles to the iCloud account already signed in on your device, so they follow you to a new iPhone or iPad. There is still nothing to sign up for and no account to create.

That data goes to your iCloud account, not to us. We cannot read it, and Apple does not give us access to it. Apple's handling of it is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

If you would rather it stayed on one device, sign out of iCloud or turn off iCloud Drive in the iOS Settings app. Puzzlr keeps working exactly the same way, with everything kept on that device. There is no equivalent on Android, where your progress always stays on the device.

Coins and the garden

Puzzlr pays out coins for the puzzles you solve, and you spend them on hints or on plants for your garden. Coins are earned by playing and by nothing else. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads, so there is nothing to buy with real money and no payment details for us to see. There is also no gambling of any kind: no loot boxes, no prize wheels, no random rewards. Every item in the shop has a fixed price and you can see exactly what you are getting before you spend.

Your coin balance and your garden are stored on your device, the same as the rest of your progress. We never receive either. On iPhone and iPad they travel with your progress through your own iCloud account, as described above.

How we use it

Only to improve the games and fix bugs. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Google acts as our analytics, crash reporting, and feedback storage provider through Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, and Cloud Firestore). Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Apple stores the iCloud data described above on your behalf. It sits in your account rather than ours, and Puzzlr cannot read it back out of iCloud on any device but yours.

Your choice

You can turn this off at any time. Open Settings inside Puzzlr and switch off "Share anonymous usage data." When it is off, the app stops sending analytics and crash data.

Feedback works differently, so the switch does not cover it. A feedback note is only ever sent because you wrote it and tapped Send. If you would rather not send one, close the prompt with "Maybe later."

iCloud sync is separate again. It follows your device's own iCloud setting rather than a switch inside Puzzlr, as described in the iCloud section above.

Data retention

Firebase keeps analytics data for a limited period under Google's defaults. Crash reports are kept while they remain useful for fixing problems. Feedback is kept while it is still useful for improving the games, and deleted once it is not.

Children

Puzzlr is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
